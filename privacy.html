In short

Carryzone works without an account and contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking services. The provider of the app receives no data from you.

Camera and videos

The camera picture is analysed on the device to follow the ball’s flight, and saved as a video of the shot in the app’s folder on your device. Saving can be switched off; older recordings are deleted automatically. None of this leaves the device unless you share a video yourself.

Location and compass

Your location is used on the device to show the landing zone on the map and to guide you there. Shots and their location are stored only on the device.

Weather

If “Weather for Flight Calculation” is switched on, Carryzone fetches wind, temperature and air pressure from Apple’s weather service (WeatherKit). It sends your location rounded to two decimal places (about one kilometre). Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Terrain heights

For the height of the terrain, Carryzone downloads elevation tiles of an open dataset from a server at Amazon Web Services. The request only names the tile you are in (about 3 km × 3 km). As with any internet request, the server sees your IP address.

Map

The map loads satellite images from Apple. Apple sees your IP address and which part of the map you are looking at. Apple’s privacy policy applies.

Subscription

Purchases are handled by Apple. The provider of the app does not learn who you are; the app only checks on your device whether a subscription is active. How many free shots you have used is stored in your device’s keychain.

Leaderboard (Game Center)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone and “Leaderboard (Game Center)” is switched on in the app, Carryzone sends your best well-measured carry of the week (a number in metres) to Game Center. Apple shows it under your Game Center name on the worldwide leaderboard. Game Center is operated by Apple; Apple’s privacy policy applies. The provider of the app receives no data from you in the process. You switch this off in the app under Settings → “Leaderboard (Game Center)”.

Support requests

If you email support, your address and the content of your message are used to reply to you and are deleted once the request has been dealt with.

Withdrawing access

You grant access to the camera, location and photos in the iOS prompts and can withdraw it at any time in your iPhone’s Settings under “Carryzone”. You switch off the weather request in the app under Settings → “Weather for Flight Calculation”, and the leaderboard under “Leaderboard (Game Center)”. You delete saved shots and recordings in the app or together with the app; the recordings are excluded from iCloud backup.

Your rights

The provider does not receive or store any personal data from you through the app, so there is nothing there to correct or delete. You remove the app’s data by deleting the app. For support requests you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
